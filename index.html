🕳️ Black Hole Mysteries

What is a Black Hole?

A region of space where gravity is so strong that light cannot escape from inside its event horizon.

Time and Gravity

Strong gravity can make time pass differently compared with places where gravity is weaker.

Sunlight

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.

Sound in Space

Sound cannot travel through the near-vacuum of space like it travels through air.

Neutron Stars

Neutron stars are incredibly dense remnants of certain massive stars.

The Moon

The Moon is slowly moving away from Earth by a few centimetres per year.
